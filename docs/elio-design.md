# Elio — character design (from the reference photo)

Replaces the v3 look ("brown hair, round glasses"). v5: the backpack in the photo is left out, and the hair is tidier. Apply in both versions: pixel sprite in 2D, toon low-poly model in 3D, same proportions/style as Werka.

- **Hair**: very dark brown, almost black (#2a1d17, highlight #4a3427). Good volume on top in soft locks swept the same way, with a clean fringe over the forehead and no stray tufts. Sides and back short (taper fade), so the head is wide on top and narrow at the ears. Ears visible.
- **Glasses**: thin metal frame, silver-gold (#d6c9a8), geometric rounded-hexagon lenses (not perfectly round), fairly large. Clear lenses with a small light glint.
- **Face**: warm light skin with a slight tan (#e8b896, shadow #c98f6e). Thin dark moustache. Small cheeky half-smile / smirk. Dark eyes looking a bit to the side.
- **Earring**: tiny silver stud in the left ear.
- **Outfit**: plain black t-shirt (#1c1c1f) with a small bright green embroidered mark on the left chest (#4ade80, just a small squiggle, no text/logo). No backpack, no straps. Dark trousers.
- **Vibe**: relaxed, mischievous, peeks out with a smirk before "Ahah! You found me!".
